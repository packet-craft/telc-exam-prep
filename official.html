Listening practice with official recordings

The exam provider telc publishes free practice tests for the Deutsch-Test für den Beruf B2 with recordings by real speakers, in the same style as the exam. They are the most realistic listening practice you can get.

The recordings and booklets belong to telc and may only be downloaded for private use, so this site cannot host them. You download them yourself; the player below then plays them from your own device. Nothing is uploaded.

1. Download a practice test

Open the telc download area and search the page for Übungstest Deutsch-Test für den Beruf B2. Two complete tests are listed under the course book „Einfach besser!“ (400 and 500). For each test, download:

  • the practice test (PDF booklet with the tasks),
  • the audio tracks (a ZIP file with five MP3s),
  • the Hörtexte (transcripts) and Lösungen (answer key).

Unpack the ZIP file so that you have the MP3 files in a folder. The same page also offers the audio for telc’s Prüfungstraining book, and the official practice test 1 (PDF) explains the format and scoring. Names and locations on the telc site can change.

2. Listen under exam conditions

Open the booklet at the listening part, choose the MP3 files below and play them in order. The tracks follow the booklet: Hören Teil 1 to Teil 4, then Hören und Schreiben.

No files chosen yet.

3. Check your answers

Compare your answers with telc’s answer key, then read the transcript for every item you got wrong and find the sentence that decides it. Each listening item is worth 3 points; 36 of 60 points is the 60 % mark.
