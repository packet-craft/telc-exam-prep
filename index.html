How to use this site

  1. Read the exam overview so you know every task type before exam day.
  2. Do a few minutes of flashcards every day. Short daily sessions work better than long weekly ones.
  3. Take a model test under exam conditions: start the timer, listen to each recording once, and check your answers only at the end.

Exam formats can change. Confirm dates, fees and current rules with your exam centre or the official exam provider.
