Exam overview: Deutsch-Test für den Beruf B2

The Deutsch-Test für den Beruf B2 (DTB B2) tests whether you can use German appropriately at a German-speaking workplace. It is about general work-related German, not the technical language of one profession. It is the exam taken at the end of a job-related language course (Berufssprachkurs) with the target level B2.

Check the current rules. This page follows the official practice test published by telc (Übungstest 1, 2022 edition), last checked on 25 November 2026. Formats, timings and rules can change. Confirm the current details, dates and fees with your exam centre or the official exam provider before your exam.

At a glance

  • Written exam: 125 minutes, 58 items in five subtests.
  • Oral exam: about 16 minutes, taken in pairs, with no preparation time.
  • Points: 240 in total; reading, listening, writing and speaking count 60 points each.
  • To pass: 60 % overall (144 points) and at least 60 % in three of the four skills.
  • Not allowed: dictionaries, phones and other electronic devices.

You have 65 minutes in one block for Lesen and Lesen und Schreiben, so you decide how to divide the time. In the listening subtests every recording is played once and the playback is not paused. In some parts you get one minute to read the items before the recording starts.

Oral exam

You take the oral exam with another candidate and two examiners. One examiner leads the conversation; the other listens and assesses. There is no preparation time.

The eight topics of Teil 1A

The two topics you are offered come from a fixed list of eight, so you can prepare all of them:

  1. An employer you have worked for or would like to work for
  2. What a good working environment looks like for you
  3. The events and experiences that shaped your choice of career
  4. A person who is a professional role model for you
  5. How to look for a job in a country of your choice
  6. What matters in a job interview in a country of your choice
  7. A product or service of your choice
  8. Your idea for starting your own business

Prepare ideas and vocabulary, not a memorised text. Examiners are told to interrupt recited speeches and steer with questions.

How writing and speaking are assessed

  • Task fulfilment is rated for each task separately: did you do what the task asks, at the right level of formality for the reader or listener?
  • Language is rated once across both written texts, and once across the whole oral exam, on three criteria. In writing: how clearly the text is organised and connected, accuracy (grammar, spelling, punctuation) and range of vocabulary and structures. In speaking: pronunciation and intonation, accuracy and range.
  • In the phone note, each piece of information is simply marked right or wrong.

What you need to pass

  • At least 60 % of all points: 144 of 240.
  • At least 60 % (36 of 60 points) in at least three of the four skills.
  • One weaker skill can be compensated, as long as its result is between 40 % and 60 %.

Preparation tips

Reading

  • In the matching parts, read the people or questions first, underline what each one needs, then scan the texts for that.
  • In Teil 3 one question has no answer. If nothing really fits, choose “x” instead of forcing a match.
  • Leave enough of the 65 minutes for the e-mail: about 20 minutes is a sensible target.

Listening

  • Use the reading time to underline key words in the items. You hear everything only once.
  • Wrong options often repeat words from the recording. Listen for the meaning, not for single words.
  • For the phone note, practise writing down names, spelled words and phone numbers at normal speed.

Writing

  • In the e-mail, cover every point your team leader lists, and keep it formal: correct greeting, polite wording, closing line.
  • In the forum post, state your opinion, give two or three reasons with examples, and use paragraphs and connectors (außerdem, deshalb, trotzdem, einerseits … andererseits).
  • The Sprachbausteine reward exactly what the flashcards train: fixed prepositions, connectors and set phrases of business letters.

Speaking

  • Prepare all eight topics with key words and a clear structure: introduction, two or three points, conclusion.
  • In Teil 1C, listen to your partner and take notes. You will have to explain one of their points.
  • In Teil 2 and 3, talk to your partner, not to the examiners. React to their ideas, ask questions and agree on a plan.
